THE FRAGMENTATION PROBLEM

Health data exists everywhere—yet personal context exists nowhere.

Smartwatches, lab PDFs, and prescriptions live in isolated silos. Without longitudinal context, individuals and clinicians miss the bigger picture.

Scattered Medical Records

Prescriptions, diagnostic lab PDFs, and discharge summaries stay buried in folders or messaging apps, making conditions, allergies, and lab trends hard to reconstruct during care visits.

Disconnected Wearable Silos

Device apps display isolated daily charts against generic population averages, ignoring activity context and failing to connect wearable shifts with medical history.

Generic, Unanchored Chatbots

Asking generic AI tools about symptoms yields broad internet answers disconnected from your actual reports, medications, allergies, and personal physiological baseline.

PRIVACY & SECURITY BY DEFAULT

Built with Data Minimization & Explicit Consent

Health records are deeply personal. MediSync applies privacy-by-design principles aligned with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023—including explicit purpose-bound consent, revocable permissions, role-separated access, and tamper-evident audit logging.

Zero PHI in QR Codes
QR codes encode only an opaque lookup ID, never names, DOB, or diagnoses.
Admin Separation
Platform administrators verify clinicians and monitor health metrics without viewing patient records.
ABOUT MEDISYNC

MediSync is an educational and personal health organization intelligence platform. We intentionally communicate data-quality limits, minimum baseline observation thresholds, and unavailable external integrations honestly—and never claim clinical diagnosis or unverified regulatory certification.
