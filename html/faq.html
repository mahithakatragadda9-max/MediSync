General
Basic information about BootUI

You can customize the dashboard layout by navigating to Settings and selecting the Layout options. From there you can choose between fixed or fluid container widths, toggle the sidebar between expanded and collapsed states, switch between light and dark themes, and adjust the header and footer visibility. Additionally, each dashboard widget supports drag-and-drop repositioning, so you can arrange your workspace exactly how you prefer.

BootUI supports all modern browsers including Google Chrome (version 90+), Mozilla Firefox (version 88+), Microsoft Edge (version 90+), Safari (version 14+), and Opera (version 76+). We recommend keeping your browser updated to the latest version for the best experience. Internet Explorer is not supported as it has reached end-of-life status.

Most data tables and charts in BootUI include an Export button in their toolbar. You can export data in multiple formats including CSV, Excel (XLSX), PDF, and JSON. For charts, you can also export them as PNG or SVG images. Simply click the export icon and select your preferred format. For bulk exports, navigate to Settings, then Data Management, and use the Bulk Export tool to download all your data at once.

Yes, BootUI is fully responsive and works seamlessly on all device sizes. The sidebar automatically collapses on smaller screens and can be toggled with the hamburger menu. All tables become horizontally scrollable, charts resize dynamically, and form layouts stack vertically on mobile devices. We've tested the dashboard on a wide range of devices including iPhones, iPads, Android phones and tablets, and various desktop resolutions.

Absolutely! BootUI is built with CSS custom properties (variables) that make theming straightforward. You can override the primary, secondary, success, warning, and danger colors in the style.css file, or use the built-in Theme Builder under Settings to visually customize your color palette. Changes are applied in real-time across all components, charts, and UI elements without needing to rebuild any files.

We release updates approximately every two weeks, including bug fixes, performance improvements, and new features. Major version updates with significant new functionality are released quarterly. All updates are backward-compatible, and we provide detailed changelogs and migration guides for any breaking changes. You can enable auto-updates in Settings or manually update through the admin panel.
Account
Account management and access

To reset your password, click the "Forgot Password" link on the login page and enter your registered email address. You'll receive a password reset link within a few minutes. If you're already logged in, go to Settings, then Security, and click "Change Password." You'll need to enter your current password and then set a new one that meets our security requirements: minimum 8 characters with at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, and one number.

Navigate to Settings, then Security, and toggle on Two-Factor Authentication. You'll be prompted to scan a QR code with an authenticator app like Google Authenticator, Authy, or Microsoft Authenticator. After scanning, enter the 6-digit code to verify setup. We also provide backup codes that you should save in a secure location in case you lose access to your authenticator app.

Yes, BootUI supports a comprehensive role-based access control (RBAC) system. Default roles include Super Admin, Admin, Manager, Editor, and Viewer, each with different permission levels. You can also create custom roles with granular permissions for specific modules and actions. Users can be assigned multiple roles, and permissions are additive, meaning the user gets the combined permissions of all assigned roles.

Go to Settings and then Team Management. Click the "Invite Members" button and enter the email addresses of the people you'd like to invite. You can assign them a role and optionally add them to specific departments or projects. Invitations expire after 7 days and can be resent if needed. Invited users will receive an email with a link to create their account and set their password.

To delete your account, go to Settings, then Account, and scroll to the Danger Zone section. Click "Delete Account" and confirm your decision by entering your password. Please note that account deletion is permanent and irreversible. All your data including projects, files, messages, and settings will be permanently removed within 30 days. We recommend exporting your data before proceeding with deletion.
Billing
Payments, plans and invoices

We accept all major credit and debit cards including Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover. We also support PayPal, Stripe, and bank transfers (ACH) for US customers. For enterprise customers, we offer invoicing with NET 30 payment terms. All payments are processed securely through our PCI-DSS compliant payment infrastructure.

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from Settings and then Billing. When upgrading, you'll be charged the prorated difference for the remaining billing period. When downgrading, the new rate takes effect at the start of your next billing cycle, and you'll retain access to your current plan's features until then. No data is lost when changing plans.

All invoices are available in your Billing settings. Navigate to Settings, Billing, and scroll down to the Invoice History table. Each invoice has a "Download" button that generates a PDF with all the details you need for your records or tax purposes. You can also set up automatic invoice forwarding to your accounting email address.

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for all new subscriptions. If you're not satisfied within the first 14 days, contact our support team for a full refund, no questions asked. After the 14-day period, refunds are handled on a case-by-case basis. We also offer a free tier so you can try the core features before committing to a paid plan.

Yes! We offer a 30-day free trial of our Business Pro plan with full access to all features. No credit card is required to start your trial. At the end of the trial, you can choose to subscribe to any plan or continue with our free tier which includes basic dashboard features for up to 3 users and 5 projects.
Technical
Technical setup and integrations

BootUI provides a built-in Integrations hub under Settings where you can connect popular services like Slack, GitHub, Google Workspace, Jira, and more with just a few clicks. For custom API integrations, we offer a RESTful API with comprehensive documentation, webhooks for real-time event notifications, and SDKs for JavaScript, Python, and PHP. API keys can be generated in Settings under the Developer section.

API rate limits depend on your plan. The Free tier allows 100 requests per minute, the Starter plan allows 500 requests per minute, and the Business Pro plan allows 2,000 requests per minute. Enterprise customers can negotiate custom rate limits. Rate limit headers are included in every API response so you can monitor your usage programmatically. If you exceed the limit, you'll receive a 429 status code with a Retry-After header.

Yes, our Enterprise plan includes a self-hosted option. You can deploy BootUI on your own infrastructure using Docker containers or traditional server setups. We provide detailed deployment guides for AWS, Google Cloud, Azure, and on-premises environments. The self-hosted version includes all features of the cloud version plus additional customization options and direct database access.

Go to Settings, Developer, and then Webhooks. Click "Create Webhook" and provide the endpoint URL, select the events you want to subscribe to (such as project.created, task.completed, user.invited), and optionally set a secret key for payload verification. You can test webhooks with the built-in testing tool before going live. Webhook delivery logs with retry information are available in the dashboard.

Yes, SSO is available on the Business Pro and Enterprise plans. We support SAML 2.0 and OpenID Connect (OIDC) protocols, making it compatible with popular identity providers like Okta, Azure AD, Google Workspace, OneLogin, and Auth0. SSO setup is available under Settings, Security, and then Single Sign-On. Our team can also assist with custom SSO configurations for enterprise customers.

BootUI supports data import via CSV, Excel, and JSON files for most modules. Navigate to any data table and click the Import button in the toolbar. We also offer direct migration tools for popular platforms like Asana, Trello, Monday.com, and Basecamp. For large-scale migrations, our Enterprise support team can assist with custom data mapping and automated migration scripts to ensure a smooth transition.
Security
Data protection and compliance

All data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption and in transit using TLS 1.3. We use enterprise-grade infrastructure hosted on AWS with multi-region redundancy. Regular security audits are conducted by independent third-party firms, and we maintain SOC 2 Type II certification. Database backups are performed every hour and stored in geographically separate locations. Our security team monitors for threats 24/7.

Yes, BootUI is fully GDPR compliant. We provide tools for data portability, right to erasure, and consent management. A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is available for all customers. EU customer data can be stored exclusively in EU data centers upon request. We also maintain compliance with CCPA, HIPAA (for healthcare customers on Enterprise plans), and other regional data protection regulations.

We have a comprehensive incident response plan in place. In the event of a security breach, affected users will be notified within 72 hours as required by GDPR. Our security team will investigate, contain the incident, and implement remediation measures. We will provide detailed incident reports and recommended actions for affected users. Our bug bounty program encourages responsible disclosure of vulnerabilities.

Yes, Business Pro and Enterprise customers can configure custom data retention policies. Navigate to Settings, Security, and then Data Retention. You can set retention periods for different data types including audit logs (1-7 years), deleted items (30-365 days), chat messages, and file attachments. Automated data purging runs on your configured schedule, and you'll receive notifications before any data is permanently deleted.
